# 2026-06-15

## Multiple Routes per Driver — Banner + initial conflict modal (Modal 4)

First working build of the driver multi-route conflict flow. When a dispatcher assigns the same driver to more than one route on the same day, the system now surfaces the conflict instead of silently over-assigning.

### Banner

An amber persistent bar appears directly below the top nav when the conflict scenario is active. It does not overlap the map — it pushes all panels (filter sheet, workspace sheet, map controls) down via a `topOffset` value computed in `page.tsx`. Copy: **"5 orders need route assignment"** + "2 drivers each have multiple routes, decide where each order goes." CTA: **Review & Assign**.

No dismiss/close — the banner stays until the conflict is resolved via the modal.

### Review & Assign modal (Modal 4 — initial)

A 960px drag-and-drop modal where the dispatcher places unassigned limbo orders into the correct route. Layout: two columns per driver — an unassigned bucket on the left, route drop targets on the right.

Key details from this first pass:

- **Progress bar** across the top that fills as orders are placed; turns green when all are assigned.
- **Order cards**: `#1F1F1F` bg, translucent teal `D` badge, `product · gal` subtitle text.
- **Route zones**: `#171717` bg, thin colored left-border accent in the route's own color, `N Orders` badge bg `#262626`.
- **Driver label**: avatar circle with initials (e.g. "MR") + name + "N of M assigned" count.
- **Confirm & Assign** gates on all orders placed; footer shows remaining count on the left.

Drag-and-drop is native HTML5 (no library). Dropping an order on a route zone assigns it; dropping back on the limbo bucket unassigns it.
