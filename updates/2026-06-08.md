# 2026-06-08

## Truck selection moved into the route card (major)

Truck and trailer selection now happens entirely from a dropdown on the route card itself — it's no longer buried inside the expanded route view. Click the truck pill and a 480px dropdown opens, right-aligned to the card.

- **Truck + trailer rows** are bordered cards showing name + specs (gal · compartments · products) with a type badge.
- **Hover a row** and the type badge swaps for an **info button**; hovering that shows a "View Details" tooltip.
- **Click the info button** to open the **Truck Details** drawer right beside it — the dropdown stays open underneath, so you can check compartments and keep editing. Clicking the button again (or closing the drawer) returns you to the dropdown without losing your place.
- **Truck search and trailer search** open as layers on top of the dropdown, so the main selection view never disappears while you search.
- **Add Trailer** is a ghost button; the same search flow handles adding a first or second trailer.

The expanded route view now starts cleanly at the hub row + order list — no duplicate truck selector.

---

## Validation — capacity warning surfaces inline on the card

Total truck capacity (L1) over/under no longer takes up a full banner below the route card. It now appears **inline** as an orange delta (↑ exceeds / ↓ below + the gallon amount) on the truck specs row, with a dotted underline and a hover tooltip reading "Exceeds / Below Truck Capacity". The same warning shows at the top of the truck dropdown.

- **Route card banners are now reserved for stop runouts (amber) and product incompatibility (red) only.** Capacity is handled inline.
- The capacity delta shows **even when a runout warning is also present** — previously it could get hidden behind the amber banner.
